Review annotation for TPB constructs

Open, reproducible measurement of psychological constructs in guest reviews.

    Everyone codes batch_00 first — it measures agreement between coders. After that, take the batch you were assigned.

    Codebook — read once before you start (5 min)

    Tick a construct when the guest actually expresses it about this stay. Do not infer what they probably felt. If the wording is ambiguous, do not tick.
